About

I started out fixing hardware and resetting passwords as an IT support intern, then moved into DevOps during my National Service at Solv Ghana — where I took a full-stack leads management platform from a local manually-managed server to a properly automated cloud deployment. That shift, from doing things by hand to building systems that do them for you, is what pulled me into infrastructure and automation.

Today I work mainly on AWS, using Terraform to provision infrastructure and GitHub Actions to automate the path from commit to production. I also picked up graphic design along the way, which turned out to be useful for thinking about layout and clarity — even in a terminal.
